Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

This application is a self-hosted personal email router and mailbox. It is designed for private, single-owner use and does not sell, track, or share personal data with advertisers.

Information We Handle

  • Google Account Information: When signing in with Google OAuth, we verify your email address to authenticate ownership. Only the authorized administrator account configured for this instance is granted access.
  • Email Data: Incoming emails received via Resend are parsed and archived in your instance's Firebase Firestore database. This data includes sender details, recipient addresses, subject lines, timestamps, and message contents.
  • Push Notification Subscriptions: If push notifications are enabled, your browser's push subscription endpoint and cryptographic keys are stored securely in Firestore to route delivery notifications.

Third-Party Services

This service interacts with the following external providers to function:

  • Google Identity Services & Firebase: For identity authentication and database storage.
  • Resend: For receiving inbound email webhooks and sending outbound emails.

Data Retention & Deletion

Emails moved to the Bin are purged automatically after 30 days. Trashed emails and drafts can be permanently removed at any time through the interface.
